# 💫 About Me:
I am a pre-final year Computer Science and Engineering student at KLE Institute of Technology, Hubballi, Karnataka, with a strong interest in building impactful technology solutions. I am passionate about Full Stack Development and Artificial Intelligence, and I enjoy developing systems that solve real-world problems through intelligent software.<br><br>During my academic journey, I have worked on projects that combine AI with practical applications. One of my major projects focuses on Assistive Technology for Empowering Visually Impaired Students, where I am building solutions that help visually impaired individuals interact with their surroundings using advanced computer vision and AI techniques. I have also worked on Grain Vision, a system designed to detect and identify foreign contaminants in grains using image processing and machine learning approaches.<br><br>Along with my academic projects, I gained industry exposure as a Full Stack Development Intern at Athreya Technologies, where I worked on developing web-based applications and understanding real-world software development practices. This experience helped me strengthen my problem-solving abilities and improved my understanding of building scalable and user-focused applications.<br><br>I have experience working with programming languages such as Python, Java, JavaScript, and C, and I continuously focus on improving my programming and problem-solving skills. I am particularly interested in integrating Artificial Intelligence with modern web technologies to create intelligent and accessible systems.<br><br>My goal is to become a Software Engineer and eventually specialize as an AI Engineer, where I can contribute to developing innovative solutions that make technology more efficient, intelligent, and beneficial for society.<br><br>I am always eager to learn new technologies, collaborate on meaningful projects, and explore innovative ideas in software development and Artificial Intelligence.
